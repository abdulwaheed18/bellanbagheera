The Royal Origins

Every kingdom needs its rulers. For us, that's Bella, Bagheera, and Nawaab. What started as a simple quest to find the best toys and treats for our discerning trio blossomed into Bella Nawaab Bagheera — a curated collection of royal treasures. We don't just sell pet products; our royal council rigorously tests each item.

Follow the Royal Court

Bella

The elegant matriarch. She favors sophisticated toys and has zero tolerance for subpar treats. Her approval is hard-won but highly respected.

Bagheera

The energetic hunter. Bagheera tests the durability of our toys. If it can survive his high-speed pounces, it earns the BNB seal.

Nawaab

The laid-back intellectual. Nawaab prefers puzzle toys and luxurious beds. He ensures our comfort collection meets royal standards.
